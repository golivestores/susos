About SUSOS

Where water vitality meets craftsmanship.

SUSOS® is the bathroom brand of Fujian Tongtong Building Materials Co., Ltd, headquartered in Nan’an — China’s renowned “Capital of Water Fittings”. Our 50-million-RMB group runs four subsidiary companies and a precision plant across Fujian, Guangdong and Anxi, delivering taps, smart toilets, vanities, bathtubs and hardware to 120+ countries — including Thailand’s Siam Global House and the United States’ Home Depot.

Every product is engineered with 58#/59# brass and 304 stainless steel cores, finished with our proprietary 10-step polishing and PVD plating process, and certified to ISO 9001:2015 (Cert. No. 35624Q10017R0M). Brass-casting capacity reaches 200 tonnes per month — built around four pillars: healthy, human-centric, smart and affordable.
